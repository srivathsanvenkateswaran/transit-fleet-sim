# Third-party notices

## Unofficial BMTC GTFS

The bundled bus route, stop, trip and shape subset comes from the
[Vonter/bmtc-gtfs](https://github.com/Vonter/bmtc-gtfs) community dataset. It
was scraped from the Namma BMTC app and describes a network operated by the
Bengaluru Metropolitan Transport Corporation. It is not an official BMTC feed.

The upstream `CITATION.cff` asks users to cite:

> Vivek Matthew, "Unofficial BMTC GTFS", version 2026.07.12, released
> 5 January 2024, https://github.com/Vonter/bmtc-gtfs

The upstream repository contained no `LICENSE` file at commit
`9b10e7bacbd5f81b5df9b2dd5de7b9d9d8b4d52c`. No licence is therefore implied
by this repository's MIT licence. Review the upstream terms before
redistributing the bundled data. `data/bundle/SOURCE.md` records the exact
snapshot and measurements used here.
